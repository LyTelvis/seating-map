How to Contribute

Launch AppSheet

Scan the project QR code or open the AppSheet app on your iPhone. Add it to your home screen for rapid access.

2

Snap & Map

When you spot a public seat, click "Add New Seat". The app automatically fetches your GPS. Take a clear photo and categorize the seating type.

3

Sync Sheet & Map

Data uploads instantly to our Google Sheet. The Apps Script processes the image, and the map updates with your styled pin.
