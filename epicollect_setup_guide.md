# Epicollect5 Integration Guide: Public Seating Tracker

This guide explains how to migrate your public seating collection project to **Epicollect5**. Epicollect5 is a free, scientific-grade mapping tool that natively captures iPhone GPS coordinates and syncs them with a public API. This eliminates AppSheet's photo compression and GPS stripping issues.

---

## Step 1: Create the Project on Epicollect5

1. Go to [Epicollect5](https://five.epicollect.net/) and log in with your Google account.
2. Click **Create Project**.
3. Name your project (e.g., `Public Seating Tracker`) and define the shortname/slug (e.g., `public-seating-tracker`).
4. Set the project access to **Public** so that your web dashboard and spreadsheet can access the data without complex authentication keys.

---

## Step 2: Design the Form
Using Epicollect's drag-and-drop form builder, construct your questionnaire with these exact inputs:

1. **Select One (Dropdown)**:
   - **Label**: `Seating Type`
   - **ID**: `seating_type`
   - **Options**: `Permanent`, `Temporary`, `Off-the-shelf`, `Site-specific`
2. **Location (GPS)**:
   - **Label**: `Location`
   - **ID**: `location`
   - **Settings**: Make it **Required**. On iPhones, this will force the user to tap "Get Location" which locks onto their exact GPS coordinates before submitting.
3. **Photo**:
   - **Label**: `Photo`
   - **ID**: `photo`
   - **Settings**: Make it **Required**.
4. **Text (Paragraph)**:
   - **Label**: `Description`
   - **ID**: `description`

Click **Save Project** when finished.

---

## Step 3: Link Epicollect5 to Google Sheets (Two Methods)

### Method A: The Zero-Code Formula (Easiest)
You can pull your data directly into Google Sheets using a single cell formula.
1. Open a blank Google Sheet.
2. In cell **A1**, enter the following formula (replace `YOUR-PROJECT-SLUG` with your project's shortname, e.g., `public-seating-tracker`):
   ```excel
   =IMPORTDATA("https://five.epicollect.net/api/export/entries/YOUR-PROJECT-SLUG?format=csv")
   ```
3. The sheet will automatically fetch and expand your data into columns.
4. **For Photos**: Epicollect will populate the `photo` column with filenames like `123_photo.jpg`. To get the absolute image URLs for Google My Maps, add a new column named `Public Photo URL` and paste this formula in row 2:
   ```excel
   ="https://five.epicollect.net/api/export/media/YOUR-PROJECT-SLUG?name=" & E2 & "&type=photo"
   ```
   *(Assuming E2 is your photo column cell)*.

---

### Method B: The Apps Script Importer (Most Robust)
If you want a stable database that parses locations into clean Latitude/Longitude coordinates for Google My Maps without formula shifts:
1. Copy the code in [apps_script_epicollect.js](apps_script_epicollect.js).
2. Go to **Extensions > Apps Script** in your Google Sheet.
3. Paste the code, replace `YOUR-PROJECT-SLUG` at the top with your Epicollect slug, and save.
4. Go to **Triggers** (clock icon), and add a trigger to run `syncEpicollectData` on an hourly or daily timer. It will fetch the API and populate your spreadsheet automatically in the background.

---

## Step 4: Map Visualization
Connect Google My Maps to your Google Sheet:
1. In Google My Maps, click **Import** and select your Google Sheet.
2. Set the map markers to point to the `Latitude` and `Longitude` columns generated by the Apps Script (or the location values from `=IMPORTDATA`).
3. Set the pin styles to group by `Seating Type`.
4. Ensure the pop-ups map to the `Public Photo URL` column to display photos.
