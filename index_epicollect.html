How to Contribute

×
1

Get the Epicollect5 App

Download the free **Epicollect5** app on your iPhone. Tap **Add Project** and search for your project slug.

2

Log on the Go

Spot a seat, tap **Add Entry**, take a photo, categorize the seating type, and click **Get Location** to query your GPS.

3

Upload to Cloud

Tap **Upload Data** in the app. Entries upload instantly, refreshing the public map and this stats counter dashboard.
