How to Contribute

×
1

Start the Telegram Bot

Search for @public_chair_tracker_bot on Telegram or click the "Add New Seat via Telegram" button, then tap /start.

2

Send Photo & Location

Snap a photo of the seat. Send it as an uncompressed file/document to auto-extract GPS coordinates from the photo's metadata, or upload a standard photo and attach the location pin.

3

Categorize & Save

Choose the seating type (Permanent, Temporary, etc.) and type any comments. The bot uploads the photo to Google Drive and appends the entry to our Google Sheet database instantly!
